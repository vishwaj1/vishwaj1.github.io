The Pod Vault Publisher

The Pod Vault Publisher is a scheduling tool operated by The Pod Vault. It publishes short vertical clips from podcasts to The Pod Vault's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, and to accounts of creators who authorize it.

What it does

  • Lets an authorized account owner sign in with YouTube, TikTok or Instagram.
  • Uploads videos the owner has prepared, with the title, description and settings the owner chooses, at the time the owner schedules.
  • Shows the owner the status and link of each published post.

It does not read private messages, does not collect followers' data, and does not post anything the account owner has not approved.
